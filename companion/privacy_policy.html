Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Companion ("the app", "we", "our") is an AI companion chat application developed by an independent developer based in Singapore. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. How Chat Works

Companion runs its AI conversation model directly on your device. By default, your messages are processed locally and are never transmitted to any server. The app downloads a one-time language model file (via a public, non-personalized download) the first time you use on-device chat; after that, conversations happen entirely on your phone.

2. Information We Collect

Companion stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server:

  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier (used only on your device; never transmitted or linked to your identity)
  • Your language preference (English / Chinese)
  • Daily message counts per companion character
  • Whether you've unlocked the paid "Memory" feature and additional companions
  • If unlocked: short, AI-generated summaries of your recent conversations with each companion character, used to give the companion continuity across sessions. These summaries are stored only on your device and are automatically limited to your last few conversations. We do not permanently store your raw chat messages — only these short derived summaries.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

3. In-App Purchases

Companion offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock additional companion characters and the Memory feature. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — we never see or store your payment or financial information. To confirm a purchase is genuine, the app sends only a purchase token and product ID (no personal information, no chat content) to a minimal verification service we operate, which checks the token against Google's servers and immediately discards it — it stores nothing. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles payment data.

4. Advertising

Companion uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (watching an ad grants bonus messages). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and device information to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's advertising privacy policy. Ads are entirely optional — you can use the app without ever watching one.

5. Notifications

If you grant permission, Companion may send an occasional local reminder notification. This is generated entirely on your device — no server sends these notifications, and you can decline or turn this off at any time in your device's notification settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Companion uses Google Play Billing (payments), Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads), and Google's Android Publisher API (solely to verify a purchase token, as described in Section 3). No other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising networks are integrated.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Nearly all app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Using "Delete All My Data" in the app's Privacy & Data settings, or
  • Going to Android Settings → Apps → Companion → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the app

If you need further assistance with data deletion, contact us at weekoon22@gmail.com.

8. Age Rating and Content

Companion is a non-explicit companion chat app intended for a general audience; it does not contain sexually explicit content. It is not intended for young children. If you believe a child has used the app inappropriately, please contact us so we can assist.

9. PDPA Compliance (Singapore)

We comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore). Companion does not collect personally identifiable data beyond what is described above. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at weekoon22@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
