Privacy Policy

Three Kingdoms Duel — Last updated: June 24, 2026

Overview

Three Kingdoms Duel is a single-player game free to download and play. This policy explains what data is collected, by whom, and how it is used.

Data We Collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information about you. Specifically, we collect:

  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, location, etc.)
  • No analytics or crash data from our own servers

Advertising (AdMob)

The free version of the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised ads, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Android device settings under Privacy → Ads.

Ads are shown at most once every 10 minutes during gameplay. Purchasing the Premium Unlock removes all ads permanently.

In-App Purchases

The app offers one optional in-app purchase: Premium Unlock ($2.99), a one-time payment that removes ads and unlocks additional game content. All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — all payment data is handled by Google under their terms of service.

Network Access

The app requires internet access solely for serving advertisements (AdMob) and processing in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). Your game progress and all gameplay happen entirely on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Game Save Data

Your game progress (cards, gold, equipment, medals) is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device, except through Android's standard system backup if you have enabled it — that backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Children

This app is rated for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. AdMob's data collection is subject to applicable children's privacy laws. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal data through this app should contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address before the change takes effect.
