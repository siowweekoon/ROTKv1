ClaraLife Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

ClaraLife ("the app", "we", "our") is a lifestyle coaching application developed by an independent developer based in Singapore. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information We Collect

ClaraLife stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server:

  • Your chosen coaching track (e.g. Energy Habits, Body Confidence)
  • Your daily lesson progress and streak
  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier (used only on your device to keep your progress; never transmitted or linked to your identity)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, health metrics, or any other personal information.

2. In-App Purchases

ClaraLife offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full 40-week journey. All transactions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC). We do not receive, process, or store any payment or financial information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about how they handle payment data.

3. Third-Party Services

ClaraLife does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or tracking tools. No data is shared with any third party.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Going to Android Settings → Apps → ClaraLife → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the app

If you need further assistance with data deletion, contact us at weekoon22@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

ClaraLife is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us so we can assist.

6. PDPA Compliance (Singapore)

We comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore). As ClaraLife does not collect personal data, no personal data is processed, stored, or transferred. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at weekoon22@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
