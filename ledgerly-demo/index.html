The problem

The owner doesn't see a payroll or tax-bill gap coming weeks out.

Generic forecasting tools track contractual due dates. For a gig-worker-dependent SME, that's the wrong clock — payroll is variable by design, and the thing that actually determines a cash crunch is when money is expected to move, not when a contract says it should.

The solution

Weekly, driver-based cash-flow forecasting, built around gig/contractor payroll specifically.

Expected pay date, not due date

Every payable and receivable is tracked by when it will actually move, not when a contract says it should.

Gig payroll as its own line

Not lumped into "vendor commitments" — driver/rider payouts get their own forecast line, because that's what actually moves week to week.

Driver-based scenarios

New client wins, driver churn, late-paying clients — toggle a scenario and the whole 8-week projection recalculates live.

How it works

Built on SGFinDex — Singapore's national read-only financial-data infrastructure. No payment initiation, no fund custody.

Without Ledgerly

Real-time transaction view (what Aspire and most business accounts already give you) — accurate about where cash is now, silent about where it's going.

With Ledgerly

The same account, plus a live 13-week forecast that shows where cash is going and why — complementary to your existing business account, not a replacement for it.
